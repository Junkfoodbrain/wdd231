Calypso Community

Purpose:

For fish enthusiasts to connect and share their love for aquatic life.

Scenarios:

Users can share photos of cool fish or showcase their aquariums designs

Users can sign up to participate in fish auction events as either a seller or a buyer

Users can get information on how to participate in local dives.
