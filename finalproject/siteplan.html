Site Plan Document

Calypso Community

The name combines a sea-inspired feel with a friendly community for fish hobbyists and aquarium enthusiasts.

Purpose:

For fish enthusiasts to connect and share their love for aquatic life.

Scenarios:

Users can share photos of cool fish or showcase their aquariums designs

Users can sign up to participate in fish auction events as either a seller or a buyer

Users can get information on how to participate in local dives.

Color Schema:

Color#1: black

Used for the header and footer backgrounds.

Color#2: Ocean Blue #028CC7

Used for the page background.

Color#3: Cool Sky #72B6E5

Used for the main content background.

Color#4: Tan #CDB190

Used for cards and boxed sections.

Typography:

Font#1: Atma, used for all headings and the site name.

Font#2: Roboto, used for body text and general reading content.
